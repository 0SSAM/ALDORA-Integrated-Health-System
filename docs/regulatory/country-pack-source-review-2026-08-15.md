
## Jordan official data source check

The Jordanian government open-data organization page referenced in research results was opened at <https://opendata.gov.jo/organization/migrated_2166?ds_year=2020>. The hosting layer returned “Request Rejected” with a support ID and no accessible dataset content. This is recorded as an access limitation, not as evidence that the datasets do not exist. No Jordan country pack was enabled and no dataset was imported.
